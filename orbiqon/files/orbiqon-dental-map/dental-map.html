The automation map for dental practices

Everything we'd automate in a US general or family practice, room by room: what each piece does, the software it lives in, and the person who approves it. Use it to plan your own setup, or to check what an agency is offering you.

03Where we'd start: follow-up

Unscheduled treatment is the first build we'd suggest for most practices, for three reasons:

  • It's revenue you already diagnosed. The patient said yes to the exam. The plan is sitting in your software.
  • It doesn't touch your phones. No AI answering anyone. Your front desk sends every message.
  • It's small. It needs read access to treatment plans and appointments in your practice software, and your message templates.
How the approval works: every Monday the front desk gets one list. Each patient has a drafted message. They edit, send or skip each one. Nothing goes to a patient until a person sends it.

04Where custom software beats off-the-shelf

Sometimes the gap isn't a missing automation, it's a missing tool. Common ones for practices:

  • A treatment plan page patients actually read: the plan, the cost after insurance and the next step on one phone screen, instead of a printout.
  • A multi-location dashboard showing unscheduled treatment, recall and claims across every office.
  • Your own product. If your practice has a process other practices would pay for, that can become software. We build MVPs too.

05The rules every build follows

No system diagnoses, gives clinical advice, or answers a patient's medical question. Pain, swelling and bleeding go straight to a person. Systems draft, check, sort and remind. Your team decides.
  • Patient data stays in your systems and in tools covered by a signed Business Associate Agreement under HIPAA, never pasted into a public chatbot.
  • Minimum necessary data. Each automation only reads what it needs for its job.
  • Every patient-facing message has a named approver, listed in the map above. The dentist approves the wording of anything clinical.

065 questions to ask any agency before you buy

  1. Does it work inside my practice software, or beside it?Ask them to show it reading your real schedule in Dentrix, Eaglesoft or Open Dental, not a demo account.
  2. Will you sign a BAA?And list every service that touches patient data.
  3. Who approves each step?If the answer is "nobody, it's fully automatic", walk away.
  4. Who fixes it when it breaks?A name and a response time, in writing.
  5. What's the smallest first build?A good partner proves one piece before selling you the rest.
